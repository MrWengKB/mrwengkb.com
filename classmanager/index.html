Class Manager

A free classroom management tool for teachers. Plan seating, manage class lists, assign roles, award points and track reading, with everything saved in your own Google Drive.

Open Class Manager Privacy Policy

What it does

  • Seating layouts: place single desks or groups of 4 or 6, then arrange students randomly, gender-balanced, or by hand, with rules like “A must not sit next to B”.
  • Class lists: paste names or import a .txt, .csv or .xlsx file.
  • Roles and points: assign class roles, award individual or group points, see leaderboards and undo mistakes.
  • Reading log: tick off books read, with weekly and monthly summaries.
  • In-class helpers: random name picker and countdown timer.
  • Student view (optional): students scan a QR card to see their own points, group and reading progress. No student accounts needed.
  • Four languages: English, 中文, Bahasa Melayu and தமிழ்.

How Class Manager uses your Google account

Teachers sign in with Google so their classes can be saved and opened on any device. Class Manager asks for:

  • Your name and email, only to show who is signed in.
  • Google Drive, limited to files Class Manager creates (drive.file). Each class is saved as a Google Sheet in a “Class Manager” folder in your own Drive. The app cannot see or change any of your other files.

Class Manager has no server of its own. Your class data stays in your Google Drive and your browser, and is never sent to the developer, sold or used for advertising. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
