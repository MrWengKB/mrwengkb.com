Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Class Manager is a free classroom tool that runs entirely in your web browser. It runs at mrwengkb.github.io/ClassManager and in the Android app. It has no server of its own and does not keep a copy of your data.

What we access

  • Your Google account email and name, only to show who is signed in on the page. They are kept in your browser, not sent anywhere else.
  • Google Drive, limited to files Class Manager creates (the drive.file permission). Your class layouts, class lists, seating plans and roles are saved as files in a folder called “Class Manager” in your own Google Drive. Class Manager cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.

Where your data is stored

Your class data is stored in your Google Drive and in your browser's local storage on your own device. It is not sent to the developer or to anyone else. You can view, share, download or delete these files in Google Drive at any time.

Student view (only if the teacher turns it on)

A teacher can turn on the student view for a class. Class Manager then keeps one extra file in the teacher's “Class Manager” folder that anyone with its link can open. It holds each student's own page (name, class, points, group and reading progress), and every student's part is encrypted with a key that is only on that student's QR card / link and in the teacher's class. Students need no account and can only view; they cannot see other students' details. The teacher can give a student a new code at any time, and turning the student view off deletes the file, so all cards stop working. The part of the link after #, which holds the key, is not sent to any server or to website statistics.

Website statistics

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. This uses cookies and does not include your class data, student names or Google Drive files.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app has an optional “Support” button. Only if you tap it, the app shows a video ad from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about the ad interaction to show and measure ads, as described in How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences. Ads never receive your class data, student names or Google Drive files. The website itself shows no ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads.

Removing access

You can remove Class Manager's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. To delete your data, delete the “Class Manager” folder in your Google Drive and clear this site's data in your browser. Full steps: Delete your account and data.

Use of Google user data

Class Manager's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to save and load your own classes (and, if you turn it on, the student view described above), and is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Contact

Questions or requests: please open an issue at github.com/MrWengKB/ClassManager/issues.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 23 日

课堂管家（Class Manager）是一个免费的课堂工具，完全在你的浏览器里运行。它没有自己的服务器，也不会保留你的资料副本。

我们会使用的资料

  • 你的 Google 账号 email 和名字：只用来在页面上显示谁已登录，保存在你的浏览器里，不会传到别处。
  • Google Drive，但仅限课堂管家自己建立的文件（drive.file 权限）：你的教室布局、名单、座位安排和职务，会保存在你自己 Google Drive 的「Class Manager」文件夹里。Class Manager 看不到、也不能修改你 Drive 里的其他文件。

资料存放在哪里

班级资料只存放在你的 Google Drive，以及你自己设备上浏览器的本地存储里，不会传给开发者或任何人。你可以随时在 Google Drive 查看、分享、下载或删除这些文件。

学生端（只有老师开启时）

老师可以为班级开启学生端。开启后，课堂管家会在老师的「Class Manager」文件夹里多保存一个「知道链接的人可查看」的文件，里面是每个学生自己的页面资料（名字、班级、分数、小组和阅读进度）。每个学生的那一份都用一个密钥加密，这个密钥只在那个学生的二维码卡片 / 链接里，以及老师的班级资料里。学生不需要账号，只能查看，也看不到其他同学的资料。老师随时可以给学生换新码；关闭学生端会删除这个文件，所有卡片都会失效。链接里 # 后面含密钥的部分不会传到任何服务器，也不会进入网站统计。

网站统计

本网站使用 Google Analytics 统计访问次数和页面使用情况。这会用到 cookie，但不包含你的班级资料、学生名字或 Google Drive 文件。

安卓应用里的广告

安卓应用里有一个可选的「支持作者」按钮。只有你点了它，应用才会播放一段 Google AdMob 的视频广告。AdMob 可能会收集你设备的广告 ID、IP 地址和广告互动资料，用来显示和衡量广告，详见 Google 如何使用使用其服务的应用中的信息。广告只限适合所有年龄的内容。广告永远拿不到你的班级资料、学生名字或 Google Drive 文件。网站本身不显示广告。你可以在手机的「设置 → Google → 广告」重设或删除广告 ID。

取消授权

你可以随时在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 取消 Class Manager 对你 Google 账号的访问权限。要删除资料，请删除 Google Drive 里的「Class Manager」文件夹，并清除浏览器里本网站的资料。详细步骤见删除账号和资料。

Google 用户资料的使用

课堂管家对 Google API 所得资料的使用，遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括其中的 Limited Use 要求。资料只用于保存和读取你自己的班级（以及你开启的学生端，见上文），绝不出售、分享给第三方或用于广告。

联系

如有问题或要求，请到 github.com/MrWengKB/ClassManager/issues 提出。
