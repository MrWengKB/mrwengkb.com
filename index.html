MrWengKB's Creations

Free web apps, learning games and Android apps made by a teacher in Singapore, for teachers, students and families. Everything runs in your browser or on your device. There is no server collecting your data.

Apps that use Google sign-in

Two apps let you sign in with Google so your work is saved in your own Google Drive and follows you to any device. Both ask only for your name, email address, and access to the files the app itself creates (drive.file). They cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Class Manager · 课堂管家

A classroom management tool for teachers: seating plans, class lists, roles, points, a reading log, a random name picker and a timer. Each class is saved as a Google Sheet in a “Class Manager” folder in the teacher's Drive.

Open Class Manager About

Honeycomb War · 蜂窝战争

A strategy game played on a honeycomb board, against the computer or a friend. Sign-in is optional: if you sign in, your level and game in progress are saved as one encrypted file in a “Honeycomb War” folder in your Drive.

Your data

The developer never receives, stores, sells or shares your data. Google user data is used only to save and load your own work, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
